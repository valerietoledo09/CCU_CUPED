CCU CUPED — PRESENTATION FOLLOW-UP & TEAM DEBRIEF

AFTER THE VISIT
1. Mark the visit Completed only after the presentation takes place.
2. Thank the teacher. Send the optional two-minute survey using the Owner
   Teacher feedback & resources section. Review the recipient and link.
3. Mark Invitation sent only after pressing Send in your email app.
4. Read replies in your school inbox; mark Reply received in your private tracker.
5. Record one practical improvement without copying unnecessary personal data.

FIVE-MINUTE TEAM DEBRIEF
- KEEP: What worked well? Name one specific example.
- IMPROVE: What confused students or needed a clearer explanation?
- TIME: Did we stay within the agreed classroom time?
- INCLUDE: Did everyone have a speaking role and a chance to contribute?
- ACT: Choose one improvement, one person responsible, and a date to review it.

BEFORE THE NEXT VISIT
- Confirm the date/time and respect any teacher who declines.
- Check room, school day, lunch, presenter availability, and materials.
- Rehearse the opening, main points, and closing together.
- Apply the previous teacher's useful feedback.

Survey responses arrive by email, not in the public calendar. The Owner tracker
is saved only in the browser where it was entered. Do not publish teacher comments
or email replies in student calendar notes.
